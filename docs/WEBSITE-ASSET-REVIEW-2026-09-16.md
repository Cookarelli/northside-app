# Northside website: product and image review

Reviewed September 16, 2026. Source: [northsidecollectibles.com](https://www.northsidecollectibles.com). The submitted spelling did not resolve; the existing project context and the Northside public site identify this domain.

## Recommendation

Use the six priority product photographs below to replace the generic shop artwork during the design pass. Keep the approved clean Northside SVG as the app logo. Reuse the dedicated grading and consignment artwork selectively, with readable app text outside the image. These are real website image references, not fictional merchandise and not yet connected to Shopify inventory or checkout.

This was a read-only review. The app, website, Shopify catalog, saved fixtures, loyalty rules and future in-store gates were not changed. Prices below are a dated observation from the existing website, not a live feed or authority for the app. Exact Square-to-Shopify product/variant mappings remain unknown.

## Available categories

The [shop landing page](https://www.northsidecollectibles.com/buy-a-box-2) has Football, Baseball, Basketball, Pokémon, NASCAR, Hockey, Soccer, a combined Star Wars/UFC/other category and Supplies. Catalog navigation also contains Northside Apparel, Group Breaks and Card Show. The latter are navigation entries; this review did not establish their current inventory. Baseball showed 27 results, Pokémon 4 and Supplies 14 at inspection; this is not an exhaustive catalog count.

The app currently shows only a small fictional category set. Add actual category support deliberately during the design/catalog pass; do not put every non-sports product under Supplies. Keep break spots and event tickets distinct from physical retail products.

The website header includes partner marks above the Northside logo. That is a different composition from the supplied clean app SVG. The approved app logo remains the default.

Product images mix portrait and landscape dimensions. Display the whole package within consistent square image areas, using contain rather than cropping. Some inspected homepage images were 320px derivatives; their source markup advertised larger variants up to 2560px, but that does not prove the original contains that much detail. Verify the larger source before preparing high-density app assets. The MLB detail image loaded at 1176 × 1192; Pokémon and supplies examples loaded around 640px wide. Banner images were around 1800–2000px wide.

For implementation, save selected approved source images with provenance, generate appropriately sized local assets, and use descriptive alt text. Do not rely permanently on a legacy website CDN. No image files were downloaded or installed in the app during this review; the reference images above load from their existing public sources.

## Content differences to resolve before adopting website copy

1. **Grading providers:** The [current grading form](https://www.northsidecollectibles.com/grading-service) lists PSA and SGC. The project still treats the earlier “BGP” reference as unconfirmed. This is evidence to reconcile with operations, not authorization to silently rename a provider. Do not import the website’s turnaround estimates as app promises.
2. **Consignment economics:** The [consignment page](https://www.northsidecollectibles.com/consignment-service) says the consignor receives the full hammer amount while Northside absorbs the listed costs. The preview’s $100 sale / $20 fees / $80 net example is explicitly fictional and should not be presented as Northside’s actual standard fee schedule. Confirm current policy before replacing the example or adding customer promises.
3. **Dated promotions:** A homepage slider still advertises a May 102% payout promotion. Do not carry that banner or rate into a September app design. Use the dedicated evergreen service banner instead.
4. **Identity of the commerce system:** The observed public website uses Square Online/Weebly assets and product routes. These product IDs are not Shopify IDs. Using a photo does not make its product purchasable in the app or verify sellable stock.
5. **Social/break content:** The homepage embeds a Northside YouTube replay and Instagram feed. The embed uses northside_collectibles while footer links use northsidesportsbreaks. Record these as discovery sources; confirm the intended current accounts/event destination. An embedded replay is not proof of a live break.
6. **App context:** Artwork depicting actual cards belongs on promotional/service surfaces. Never attach it to a fictional customer's grading or consignment record as if Northside holds that customer's card. Real image references also require updating the current “all products are fixtures” wording if they become real product reference cards in the preview.

## Implementation order for the design pass

1. Preserve the clean supplied logo and apply consistent Northside blue/black/white styling.
2. Replace generic shop artwork with the six selected, correctly named product references. Keep them clearly labeled as website references with purchases disabled until mapped to the authoritative catalog; avoid pairing a real image with a made-up product name or price.
3. Add category discovery matching the real catalog and build service cards using the two reusable banners.
4. Resolve provider and consignment copy with the owner before publishing business promises. Keep dated offers out.
5. When Shopify is configured, verify exact product/variant identity and source price, availability and checkout from Shopify. Preserve existing products, inventory pools and disabled store features.
