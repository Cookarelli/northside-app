## Three implementation passes

### Ledger, ownership and earning

Migration 006 adds the `northside_loyalty` role and extends the original accounts, ledger, rules, rewards, tier qualification, reservations and voucher tables. New tables store normalized order snapshots, frozen line allocations, leased jobs, voucher paid usage, reviews, private audit, immutable restoration entries and independent checkout clearances. Compound tenant/customer keys, RLS and explicit application ownership filters all remain in effect. The new worker cannot read authentication sessions, customer tokens or operational card/consignment records. Customers only receive their wallet; staff projections remain private. Owner/admin mutations are reasoned and audited; operations/read-only can inspect reports; content editors cannot access loyalty operations.

A verified Shopify mapping is required for enrollment. Order ownership is an exact configured shop/customer mapping, never email, order number or a cart claim. Identifiable POS and online purchases use the same account. Unknown customers enter review. An authenticated owner may request rechecking an independently verified owned order, but cannot claim an anonymous order by typing its number. Staff must establish Shopify ownership and re-read current provider data. Rule launch and original enrollment dates still apply: no automatic prelaunch, pre-enrollment or Square backfill.

Economics are immutable versions with approver/effective time. Approval creates a new version instead of editing the draft. Live approval requires the exact invited staff UUID privately verified as Joey in `LOYALTY_APPROVER_STAFF_ID`; it has no default. New live versions require a future effective timestamp. Rewards start inactive and have their own recorded approval. No production rule/reward has been approved in this delivery.

Supported policies are deliberately validated: 1–1,000 whole points per eligible USD dollar; four distinct increasing tiers (Rookie/Vet/HOF/GOAT defaults); a 1–3,650-day rolling qualification window; explicit product registry of up to 100 IDs and product exclusions; no point expiry; original-period qualifying-spend reduction on returns; manual reward-point restoration. Service categories require explicit inclusion and exact product IDs; unknown merchandise and gift cards do not earn. Other point expiry, calendar-tier, stacking or automatic-restoration policies require implementation and verification before activation.

Initial line points are `floor(original eligible net cents × points per dollar / 100)`. Current eligible cents are capped at the original allocation after cumulative line refunds, removed quantities and Shopify’s current post-discount pre-tax line total. Refund reversal is `floor(original points × cumulative removed cents / original cents)`; target points are original points minus that reversal. Reconciliation appends only the difference from the already-posted target. This prevents repeated small refunds from accumulating rounding loss and caps full reversals at the original award. Per-line eligibility/rule snapshots survive later catalog changes. Removed lines/cancellation reverse their remaining earnings. Increased existing-line amounts/quantities and malformed line edits enter review before any partial posting; additional payment evidence and an audited correction are required for unsupported increases.

A database AFTER INSERT trigger updates the cached balance only for actual new ledger entries; an `ON CONFLICT` no-op cannot change it. Financial operations compare cache with the signed ledger under an account lock. Staff corrections are new entries with actor/reason/source UUID. Debt is retained if a refund follows spending; spendable is `max(0, ledger − held/review reservations)`, and negative accounts enter review. Tier qualification uses net eligible spend in its own rolling window, independently of spendable points; each reconciled purchase appends a qualification snapshot. The current wallet also recalculates the window as time passes.

### Shopify issuance and recovery

The customer requests only a server reward ID plus stable request UUID. A transaction locks the account, checks the authenticated verified customer, active approved rule/reward, ledger/cache and spendable points, then inserts an immutable reservation snapshot, high-entropy alphanumeric code and outbox job together. Concurrent/repeated requests cannot allocate the same available points. The code is `NS` plus 40 uppercase random hexadecimal characters (160 random bits).

The separate worker uses a two-minute lease, durable backoff and at most eight automatic attempts. An old lease cannot commit. Every issuance first queries the stable code. Missing evidence permits creation; timeout, unknown response or conflict retains the same code/hold. Exact confirmed evidence atomically posts the permanent exchange debit, maps the voucher and completes the reservation/job. A proven initial creation rejection followed by confirmed absence can release a hold. A later rejection cannot erase prior uncertainty. Exhausted/contract-mismatched jobs go to staff review. Staff retry retains uncertainty history. Worker output counts attempted jobs and caught failures, not successful issuance; inspect persisted job state for the actual outcome.

Fixed rewards use USD integer cents, 1–365-day expiry, a minimum eligible spend at least the full reward value, exact eligible products OR collections (up to 100 IDs), one use and one eligible Shopify customer. Product/order stacking is rejected; a shipping combination can be an explicitly approved term. The adapter checks exact code, customer context, amount, minimum, product/collection scope, start/end, combinations and usage restrictions. Truncated eligibility or a mismatched response is not issuance confirmation.

The Admin contract uses `discountCodeBasicCreate`, `DiscountCodeBasicInput.context.customers.add`, `codeDiscountNodeByCode` and `discountCodeDeactivate`; deprecated `customerSelection` is not used. Installed `write_discounts` access is checked before discount worker calls. Admin credentials remain server-only through the supported Prompt 3 installation/token path; do not assume the connected account has those scopes. API version stays pinned to 2026-07. Actual grant/access/checkout behavior remain unverified.

The cart route looks up an owned, issued, unexpired voucher from its server ID, binds the verified customer to the existing cart, applies the code with `cartDiscountCodesUpdate` and reads applicability/warnings from Shopify. A temporarily ineligible cart leaves the voucher visible. An existing other applicable cart code is not silently replaced. Both purchase and redemption public gates must be open, so this route currently returns not launched. Other-customer, minimum, stacking and supported checkout behavior must be tested in real Shopify before launch.

Usage is recorded separately from issuance using verified paid order discount allocations. Unknown owner, voucher reuse or late paid usage after an unused-cancellation restoration enters review. Cart abandonment, asynchronous usage count zero or expiry cannot return points. Cancellation requests enqueue deactivation; the adapter must confirm the matching code is expired/deactivated (Shopify may shorten its end timestamp). Unused restoration additionally requires staff’s recorded independent pending/paid checkout reconciliation reference, no known usage and no pending order jobs. A remaining race stays unresolved. Used-reward refund restoration requires verified usage plus current refund/cancellation evidence. All restorations are authorized manual entries with a reason, stable source UUID and total cap equal to the original exchange debit; partial manual credits share that cap. No automatic full/partial reward restoration or cash-out is enabled.

The internal `futurePosRewardUrl` helper prepares `https://9i3hnb-jw.myshopify.com/discount/{CODE}` using only validated voucher codes. It is separate from product/location QR records. No reward QR, scanner or staff POS redemption surface is exposed. Real POS customer eligibility and scanning behavior must be verified before that later feature; POS earning can be considered independently of scanning.

### Wallet, tiers and reporting

The customer wallet distinguishes sign-in required, not launched, not enrolled, loading, error, zero, debt, holds and issued/used/expired/deactivated voucher states. The most recent 100 ledger entries and reservations/vouchers are shown; balances use complete SQL totals. Staff lists are bounded to 50 rules, 100 rewards/jobs/reviews/vouchers and 500 members. Larger history pagination is not implemented. A sample account switch clears the previous owner’s visible data before loading the next result. Customer requests cannot set a live actor or override cost.

Report periods are UTC start-inclusive/end-exclusive, at most 366 days; display dates use America/Chicago. Metrics define their source and period, with current holds and current tier snapshots explicitly distinguished from historical ledger totals. Observed discount use is gross allocation on verified paid orders before refund adjustments, not issued face value or net accounting expense. Optional exposure is positive outstanding points times an explicitly entered cents-per-point assumption; it includes holds, excludes already-issued vouchers, and is not an accounting liability. See MARKETING-HUB-LOYALTY-CONTRACT.md for aggregate cohort limits and non-causal comparisons.

## Hosted setup and required external verification

Do not enable live rewards by changing an environment boolean alone. Public gates remain hard false in `lib/policy.mjs`. No real credentials are required for the preview.

1. When account access returns, complete the hosted identity/database/commerce prerequisites in SETUP-PROMPT-2.md and SETUP-PROMPT-3.md. Review all migrations 001–006 on an authorized staging database, back up existing data, then run `pnpm db:migrate` using migration credentials privately. Applied migration checksums are immutable; corrections need a forward migration. Never run fixtures on the hosted database.
2. Provision the dedicated `northside_loyalty` login privately with TLS and only the defined role; set `LOYALTY_DATABASE_URL` on the worker. The web runtime retains `northside_runtime`; auth retains `northside_auth`; the commerce worker retains `northside_commerce`. Never use a service/migration role as runtime. Verify actual hosted RLS, connection pooling, transaction/advisory locks and multiple simultaneous workers, then backup/restore/restart.
3. Verify Joey’s actual invited staff membership and configure its exact UUID; obtain and record real economic approval/effective date. Seed no economic defaults into production. Inspect product categories and exclusions with approved exact IDs. Keep Shopify products Draft and inventory/location/fulfillment settings unchanged; any selected test catalog requires separate authorization.
4. Confirm authorized Admin order/discount access, Storefront cart/customer access, webhook subscriptions and current API fields against the real installed app. Supported order reads are bounded to 250 lines, fewer than 100 refunds and fewer than 250 order transactions; refund connections must be complete. Truncated/malformed reads retry and eventually enter review. Tax-inclusive, test, subscription, pending/failed/ambiguous monetary-refund and unsupported payment states preserve points in review. This delivery does not certify those checkout modes. Restrict launch to verified supported configurations.
5. Run the separately authorized checkout matrix in LOYALTY-RECONCILIATION.md. Record actual order/discount/evidence IDs privately, exact app scopes/configuration, dates and outcomes. Do not treat mocks or browser emulation as these results. Shopify test-mode orders intentionally do not earn through the live loyalty reader; a controlled staging fixture/test process must not weaken production gates. No actual checkout has been run yet.
6. Only after the required test evidence passes may `SHOPIFY_LOYALTY_CHECKOUT_VERIFIED=true` be recorded on the worker. This is an additional safeguard, not permission or a public feature switch. Separately review launch gate changes and their tests. Plan scheduled bounded `pnpm orders:work` then `pnpm loyalty:work` passes with monitoring of oldest pending/review job, expired leases, negative balances, cache mismatches and unknown discounts. No scheduler or alert destination is installed. Queued prelaunch orders remain ineligible by rule/enrollment date.
7. Use audited forward corrections for financial mistakes; never delete a ledger entry, voucher mapping or original allocation. Pause new earning/redemption and suspend affected rewards during an incident, preserve queued work, reconcile provider evidence before resuming, and verify a restored database against Shopify’s surviving vouchers before reopening. See the recovery report for uncertain issuance and late-payment procedures.
